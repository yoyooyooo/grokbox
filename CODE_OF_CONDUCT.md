# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the grokbox community free from harassment
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable technical feedback, protect
private data, and accept maintainer decisions about safety and project scope.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, credential disclosure,
and testing systems without authorization are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the contact process in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) when public discussion would expose a person or
sensitive information. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and avoid
publishing unnecessary personal information.
